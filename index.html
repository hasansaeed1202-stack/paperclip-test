                Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?
              
              

                No. The form prepares a WhatsApp request. You must send it in
                WhatsApp. The clinic checks the doctor’s availability and
                confirms your time or offers an alternative.
              

            

            

              How much does an online consultation cost?
              

                The base fee is PKR 1,000 for
                20 minutes, whether you choose
                WhatsApp voice, WhatsApp video or a normal phone call.
              

            

            

              How do I pay?
              

                The clinic will arrange payment around appointment confirmation
                using Easypaisa, Allied Bank or UBL.
                Contact the clinic for the actual payment details.
              

            

            

              What is free on Friday?
              

                The in-person consultation/checkup fee is waived every Friday.
                Medicines are charged separately. This offer does not waive the
                online consultation fee.
              

            

            

              Can I visit without booking online?
              

                Yes. For a physical visit, call, WhatsApp or come to the clinic
                and obtain a token. Hours are
                Monday–Saturday · 9:00 AM–5:00 PM.
              

            

            

              Can medicines be delivered to me?
              

                Courier delivery within Pakistan may be arranged after
                consultation, with courier charges paid by the patient.
                International medicine delivery is subject to availability,
                courier service and destination-country regulations.
